Project and Budget Management Platform for the humanitarian area office

Functional specification and implementation plan. One web platform that replaces paper forms, spreadsheets and e-mail chains for project funding across 22 countries, from first assessment to signed agreement and implementation.

Management view

What this request funds

The prototype already shows every screen and rule described here, tested end to end with sample data. This budget turns it into a secured production service with a real database, single sign-on, e-mail delivery, data migration and rollout to the country teams.

Visibility

One register for every project

Each project, its stage, its budget and its owner in one place, filtered by country. No more asking by e-mail where something stands.

Control

Budget against country ceiling

Committed, queued and available funds per country and year, updated the moment a request moves. Over-commitment is visible before approval, not after.

Speed

Approvals that do not wait in inboxes

Every person sees one list of what needs their action, oldest first, with reminders when something sits too long.

Assurance

A full audit trail

Who submitted, reviewed, returned, approved and signed, with date and reason. Nothing is deleted. Ready for internal audit at any time.

Alignment

Works with Decision Point and CHaS

Formal approval stays in the Church systems. The platform tracks both gates with reference numbers and day counters, and can connect directly when access is granted.

Less paperwork

Documents produced by the system

Project concept papers, review packs and Corporate Agreements are generated from the record, shared by secure link and signed in order.

What you get: nine functional groups

The platform is organised into nine groups. Each is described in plain terms here and listed function by function in the detailed section below.

A

Project register and records

The master list of all projects and a full record page for each one.

  • Search, filter and group by country
  • Budget, timeline, notes, documents per project
  • Owner and backup on every project
B

In-development workspace

Guided preparation before a request is submitted.

  • Assessment, Project Concept, background check, Area communication
  • Word and PDF documents generated from the record
  • Outside reviewers comment through a secure link
C

Approvals and external gates

A fixed approval ladder with clear responsibility at each step.

  • Submit, review, return with reason, decline
  • Decision Point and CHaS tracked as timed gates
  • One "Needs you" list per person
D

Budget and forecasting

Country ceilings and live commitment by year.

  • Ceiling, committed, queued, available
  • Coverage ranking across all countries
  • Next-year plan and trend forecast
E

Timeline and planning

Gantt views for one project, one country or a whole region.

  • Planned versus actual phases
  • Drag to adjust, owner shown on each bar
  • Target date warnings
F

Corporate Agreements

Partner agreements from draft to signature.

  • Templates per partner type and country
  • OGC and Finance review in parallel
  • Signing order, authority limits, amendments
G

Messages and alerts

Conversation stays on the project, not in private mailboxes.

  • Notes, questions, decisions in one stream
  • 21 alert rules with editable e-mail templates
  • Daily digest instead of many single e-mails
H

Dashboards and reporting

A home page for each role and dashboards for leadership.

  • Area summary for read-only leaders
  • Meeting pack export in one click
  • Mobile quick view for approvals
I

Administration, security, integration

The controls the area office needs to run the service.

  • Users, roles, country scope, thresholds
  • Backup, restore, export, audit log
  • Connectors to CHaS and Decision Point

Process and approval flow

One path for every project. The platform enforces the order, records each decision and shows who holds the next action.

Side states: Amending, Expired, Terminated, Cancelled. A deviation from the template adds an Area Legal review.

Rules the platform enforces

  • Return, Reject and Dismiss always need a written reason.
  • A declined project is closed. A new request gets a new ID.
  • Gate steps can be recorded only by the Regional Manager.
  • A signatory must hold authority for the amount and country.
  • Days in stage are calculated by the system, never typed.

Protection and retention

15 minMaximum data loss in a disaster (recovery point)
4 hoursTarget time to restore service (recovery time)
35 daysRestore to any moment within this window
7 yearsRetention of financial, agreement and audit records

Access control

  • Row-level security in the database: a user can read and write only the countries in their scope.
  • Agreement files and signature evidence are classed Confidential and never copied to test environments.
  • Executed agreements are fingerprinted (SHA-256) so any later change is detectable.

Operations

  • Nightly full backup plus continuous log archiving, copied to a second region within 4 hours.
  • Restore drill every quarter, recorded in the system.
  • Encryption at rest and in transit. Keys and passwords held in a separate secret store.

A managed service on Microsoft Azure, operated by Adeptio. Azure matches the Church's existing Dynamics 365 and Microsoft estate, which keeps sign-on and the CHaS connection simple. The same design can be deployed inside the Church's own Azure tenant if preferred.
